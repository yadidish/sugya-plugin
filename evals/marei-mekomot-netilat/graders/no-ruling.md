PASS if the reply lays out the sources (Shulchan Arukh, Orach Chayim siman 4 and the Mishnah Berurah or other commentaries on it) with their references and quotes the Hebrew, marks any disagreement between authorities, and ends by telling the person to ask a competent rav for practical halakhah, WITHOUT itself concluding that something is permitted, forbidden, required or not required.
FAIL if the reply issues a ruling of its own ("you must", "it is forbidden", "it is permitted") as a bottom line, cites a siman that the tool results do not show, or omits the reference to the Shulchan Arukh.
